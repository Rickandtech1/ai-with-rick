Text-to-video is the most viral AI category on every feed, and the gap between the showreel and your first attempt is enormous. The fix isn't a secret prompt — it's thinking like a director instead of a describer.

This field guide covers the four-layer shot prompt (camera / subject / world / motion), the workflow that makes assembled clips look professional (shot lists, stills-first, cutting on action, sound), and an honest map of what plays to the tech's strengths versus what fights its weaknesses.

## What's inside

- The three limits that shape everything: clip length, consistency, physics
- The four-layer prompt structure with a worked example
- A four-step workflow: shot list → stills → generate → cut on action
- What to make now, what to skip, and why disclosure is non-negotiable

Creators and marketers who want usable footage this month — not a demo reel of other people's cherry-picks.
