Running AI on your own machine sounds like a weekend project. It's actually about fifteen minutes — if someone hands you the right shortlist instead of a rabbit hole.

This one-pager is that shortlist: the install (Ollama, or LM Studio if you'd rather skip the terminal), a RAM-to-model table so you pull something your laptop can actually run, and an honest scorecard of what local models do well and where the cloud still wins.

## What's inside

- The 15-minute setup, terminal and no-terminal versions
- The RAM table: 8 GB to Mac Studio class, with what each tier feels like
- What locals are honestly good at (privacy, cost, tinkering) and bad at
- Three upgrades when the basics feel good: local RAG over your files, scripting the local API, coding models

## Who it's for

Anyone with sensitive documents, a curiosity itch, or a laptop and a free evening.
