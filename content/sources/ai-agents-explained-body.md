"Agent" is this year's most abused word in tech. Strip the marketing and it means something specific: an AI that acts — tools plus a try-observe-adjust loop — instead of just answering.

This guide gives you the working vocabulary: the three-part anatomy every real agent shares, where agents genuinely deliver today (coding is the standout), where they still faceplant, and a trust ladder for handing one actual work without handing it your credit card.

## What's inside

- The anatomy: brain, tools, loop — and the question that exposes fake "agentic" products
- What agents are genuinely good at in 2026, and the three places they still fail
- The trust ladder: watch mode → bounded autonomy → trusted routine
- Four questions that cut through any agent sales pitch

## Who it's for

Anyone deciding whether to let AI act on their behalf — or being sold something "agentic" this quarter.
