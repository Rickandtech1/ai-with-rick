# The 2026 AI Tool Stack for Creators

*The short list that covers a solo creator's whole pipeline — and the categories where paying for two tools is paying twice. From the [AI with Rick](https://ai-with-rick.vercel.app) library.*

## One tool per job

| Job | What to look for | Trap to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Writing & thinking | One frontier chat assistant you use daily until it knows your voice | Subscribing to three chatbots that do the same thing |
| Scripts → shorts | Long-video-to-clips tool with auto-captions | Tools that promise "viral" clips — the hook is your job |
| Thumbnails & graphics | Image model + a template you reuse | Regenerating from scratch every time; consistency beats novelty |
| Voice | AI voiceover or cleanup of your own recording | Cloning a voice that isn't yours without disclosure |
| Video generation | Short b-roll and transitions from text | Trying to generate whole videos — audiences can tell, and retention shows it |
| Editing | AI rough-cut, silence removal, jump-cut assembly | Letting auto-edit choose your pacing; review at 1× once |
| Repurposing | One long piece → newsletter, carousel, shorts | Posting identical text everywhere; each platform needs its own hook |

## The stack rules

1. **Adopt one tool per row, master it for a month, then evaluate.** Tool-hopping is the most expensive hobby in the creator economy.
2. **Your voice is the moat.** Use AI for volume and polish, never for the opinions. Audiences unsubscribe from generic exactly once — by never noticing you existed.
3. **Templates over generations.** The 10th thumbnail from a locked template outperforms the 100th fresh generation. Consistency is a growth strategy.
4. **Disclose synthetic media** (cloned voices, generated people). Trust is compounding; one "wait, was that AI?" moment costs months of it.
5. **Own the archive.** Export everything to your own storage monthly. Tools die; your library shouldn't die with them.

## A sane monthly budget

- **$0 tier** — frontier chat free plans + built-in phone editing covers a surprising amount. Start here; upgrade on friction, not FOMO.
- **~$40–60/mo** — one paid assistant + one clips tool + one image tool. This is the sweet spot for most solo creators.
- **$100+/mo** — only when a specific tool provably pays for itself in time saved. Run the math on one month before renewing.

## The weekly workflow that actually compounds

1. **Monday:** one long-form piece (video or essay) — human thinking, AI polish
2. **Tuesday:** AI repurposes it into 3–5 shorts + a newsletter draft — you rewrite the hooks
3. **Daily, 20 min:** reply to comments yourself. No AI. This is where the audience is actually built.
